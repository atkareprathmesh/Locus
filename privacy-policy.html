Locus Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Locus is a new-tab extension that helps you organize bookmarks, notes, tasks, habits, journal entries and reminders. Locus has no account system, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated server.

Information Locus handles

Locus handles your Chrome bookmarks, your name, notes, tasks, habits, journal entries, settings, reminders, imported files and optional wallpaper images. This information is stored locally in your Chrome profile using Chrome's extension storage and bookmark APIs. Locus does not read your browsing history, top sites or page content.

How information is used

Locus uses this information only to display and operate the features you choose: showing bookmark boards, saving your workspace, displaying reminders while Locus is open, and creating manual JSON backups. Locus does not sell, rent, profile, or use your information for advertising.

Sharing and retention

Locus does not transmit or share your locally stored workspace data with the developer or other parties. Your data remains in your Chrome profile until you delete it, reset Locus, uninstall the extension, or remove the relevant Chrome data. Backups remain in your Downloads folder until you delete them.

Security and choices

Locus uses Chrome's storage and bookmark APIs. You can reset Locus from Settings, manually export a backup, or delete the extension's stored data through Chrome.

Contact

For questions or privacy requests, use the contact method listed on the Locus project page.
